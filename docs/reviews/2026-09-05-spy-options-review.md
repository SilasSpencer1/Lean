# Review of the SPY options research and paper platform

Reviewed 2026-09-05 against repository commit `3c6ef2e7c1ad90948a3b41aac5a98839e2cd4308`, branch `design/spy-options-paper-v1`.

Both original documents were read completely: [system design](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-spy-options-paper-system-design.md), 264 lines, and [Phase 1 plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-05-options-lab-core.md), 1,572 lines. This review treats Phase 1 as the first part of a larger platform. It neither implements trading nor claims that future tests have passed. Findings refer to the original documents at commit `3c6ef2e7c1ad90948a3b41aac5a98839e2cd4308`. After user approval, the originals were marked historical and the reviewed replacements were adopted as v2 documents; implementation proceeds in separate increments.

## 1. Executive verdict

**The architecture is coherent and worth implementing as an experiment, but the current interfaces should not be frozen or treated as sufficient for paper orders.** The shared Python core, separate LEAN/Alpaca adapters, fixed one-contract risk, cash champion, conservative long-option economics and shadow-only news/social are sound. The design is scientifically testable after correcting its target, data-availability contracts and validation protocol. It is safely paper-only as an intention; endpoint confinement, stale-order protection, recovery and exit authority still require implementation and tests. It is realistically implementable in phases. Its economic thesis cannot be tested credibly on indicative or minute-only option data.

The five most important changes are:

1. **Establish an admissible data contract before model development.** Separate event time, availability, receipt, retrieval and revision; preserve quote-side age, bar intervals, Greek provenance, expired-contract history and deliverables. Obtain endpoint-level proof of the historical bid/ask data and usage rights needed for the experiment.
2. **Train/evaluate the policy that will actually run.** A quote at t followed by a bid at t+30 is not the same outcome as a delayed fill followed by a 30-minute holding period, safety exits and cancellations. Use an execution-policy target; retain no-fills and censored exits. Never use simulated paper fills as training truth.
3. **Close the entry and recovery boundaries.** Generate candidates inside a single shared entry path, bind intents to current context/account/config, revalidate and reserve at submission, deduplicate broker events, latch risk halts and preserve unexpected positions for recovery. Entry failures must not disable exits.
4. **Replace subjective promotion with a preregistered experiment.** Freeze the search budget, feature/cost definitions, chronological session splits, label intervals, uncertainty procedure, untouched final period, meaningful observation floors and economic falsification rules. Compare actual portfolio daily dollar P&L, including cash days.
5. **Separate core, operational paper and economic gates.** Free data can prove plumbing; paper can test operations; neither automatically promotes an economic model. Preserve all existing caps, including one contract throughout v1. Make the intended virtual capital explicit: the plan's $5.10/$1 fixture needs at least $102,200 under its original fee-only 0.5% cap, and more once adverse execution is reserved.

The most consequential feasibility finding is narrower than “paid data required”: the advertised Alpaca history start does not imply access to historical option quotes. A paid subscription also does not, by itself, prove that a needed endpoint or permitted use exists. The inspected SDK/docs and a dated staff response support an explicit quote-history readiness gate. See [platform matrix](2026-09-05-platform-evidence.md).

## 4. Recommended design: six falsifiable choices

The [adopted system design](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-spy-options-paper-system-design-v2.md) is the complete normative replacement. The following states the research decisions and why they matter.

**Information.** Require completed causal SPY bars, fresh SPY/option quotes, independently timed delta/IV, standard-contract reference history, versioned sessions and current account/order facts. Quotes are immutable price observations, not objects that require valid Greeks merely to exist. Data about operational state enforce controls but do not enter the alpha vector. Optional skew, term structure, IV/realized-volatility comparison, extra Greeks and order flow receive separate schema/ablation trials. News/social remain shadow-only; revised macro values, hindsight-adjusted strike comparisons, future completeness and untimestamped derived values are excluded.

Define features mathematically before coding: close-to-close log returns; unannualized square-root summed squared minute returns; exact session traded-dollar/volume VWAP if available or a distinctly named proxy; same-time-of-day volume normalization fitted only from prior training data. Require 31 same-session closes for a 30-return window; at exactly 10:00 the longest feature may still be warming up. This is valid cash, not a reason to consume a bar before its availability. Price and strike units stay on the same raw basis. Maintain SPY distribution/corporate-action reference dates as known then; adjusted deliverables are ineligible.

**Outcome.** The estimand is conditional expected P&L of an attempted one-contract policy, normalized by the ask-based capital reservation. If L is the decision ask, K=100L, A is the simulated post-latency entry ask at or below L, and B the policy exit bid, then:

`Y_attempt = [100*(B-A) - fees - extra execution charges] / K`.

An observed no-fill attempt is zero; unknown/missing execution is censored. Retain filled-premium return with denominator100A separately. This avoids confusing a forecast about an order attempt with a forecast conditional on a fill selected using future information. Labels identify every price, time, fee, policy and information cutoff. Train from policy replay, not broker-simulated paper fills. A quote-at-t/quote-at-t+30 diagnostic remains useful for decomposition, but cannot pass economic promotion by itself.

Base entry uses the first admissible post-latency ask no higher than the fixed original ask limit; order lifetime ends at decision+five seconds, with cancellation taking precedence at equality. Submission must remain within the entry window. Base latency is initially one second; adverse latency is three seconds. Ten-second entry stalls necessarily expire; severe exit stalls are also tested on already-filled positions. These are declared simulation assumptions pending measurement. Exit triggers at confirmed-fill+30 minutes or earlier shared safety. An observed zero bid is a valuation/liquidity fact, not an executed zero-price sale. Promotions must pass zero-liquidation-value sensitivity for every censored filled or possibly-filled attempt, including reserved costs and subsequent occupied/uncertain state that blocks later entries until reconciliation. This is conservative accounting, not invented market data.

At decision time, reserve only causally available costs: `adverse_reserve=max(0.005*K,100*current_option_spread)` or a higher frozen training-derived estimate. The retrospective adverse scenario additionally charges `max(0.005*K,100*0.5*(entry_spread+exit_spread))` and fully replays its own latency/fills/exits; future exit spread never enters a decision or reservation. Severe doubles that charge and injects stalls; its purpose is control/loss stress, not mandatory positive P&L. Premium risk and available cash cover K plus the fee floor/estimate plus the causal adverse reserve. Verify tick size and round conservatively before revalidating the submitted price.

**Call, put or cash.** Keep 7–21 DTE, |delta|0.40–0.60, strict positive uncrossed quotes and existing spread/premium caps. Select one per right before predicting. Rank by delta distance, spread, DTE, canonical identity. Compare conservative expected **dollars** because quantity is fixed, rather than inadvertently optimizing percentage return on different premiums. A 2% forecast on $100 and 1% on $500 mean $2 and $5 before additional uncertainty/costs. This arithmetic motivates the change; no performance benefit is asserted.

Start with cash, fixed five-minute SPY momentum/reversion under identical execution and risk, then a small regularized linear attempted-return model. Freeze threshold search and any ridge grid before testing. A histogram gradient-boosted model is a single later challenger; deep learning/RL/LLM/signature approaches are excluded from v1. Preserve the existing adverse-return floor0.005. Score `K*(mean_return - threshold - uncertainty_penalty) - incremental_adverse_dollars`; all components have named units and costs are not double-counted. If no score is strictly positive, both scores tie, or uncertainty is unavailable, choose cash.

Calibration uses a separate validation tail after hyperparameter/threshold selection, with at least 50 observed attempted outcomes across 20 distinct sessions per supported forecast bucket. Preregister two-session calibration blocks and require at least ten complete such blocks; insufficient support or a nonfinite/degenerate resampling distribution means cash, never a zero uncertainty penalty. Use simultaneous one-sided bounds on mean overprediction in return units, converted to dollars by K. The 5/10/20-session sensitivity below applies to the longer portfolio P&L series, not this one-month tail. This is uncertainty about a bucket mean, not guaranteed coverage of an individual option payoff. Report mean prediction versus outcome, coverage, MAE and calibration error for all attempts and the traded subset. If a probability challenger is added, assess proper probability calibration/Brier/log loss separately; classifier accuracy is not P&L.

**Closing.** Shared policy receives clock events between entry slots. Thirty minutes from confirmed fill is the normal exit; data/model/broker failure, kill switch and daily loss retain early-exit authority. Begin reconciliation 15:35, escalate safely 15:39, demand flat 15:40; early-close recovery advances all times. Cancellation is not confirmation of zero exposure. Reconcile order lineage and long quantity before replacements or liquidation. Keep exits running during model failure or audit problems. A halt/outage can defeat timely liquidation: the correct record is a failed gate and preserved exposure, not “always flat” by assertion.

**Capital.** Retain exactly 1 contract, <=0.5% premium+cost, <=3 entries, daily halt<=-1%, drawdown halt>=5%, no borrowing. Bind a current account/mark snapshot, virtual cash reservation and session counters to each approval. Risk entry permission expires; a previous approval is not a standing order entitlement. One-contract sizing cannot be reduced except to cash. Session loss and drawdown crossings latch. Never reset the high-watermark by restarting. Portfolio drawdown uses conservative marked equity, including closing costs; stale valuation reserves full premium loss until reconciled. Explicit capital admission prevents the $200,000 test fixture from silently determining feasibility. The $5.10/$1 fixture's lower bound rises from $102,200 to $102,710 with the minimum adverse reserve, or $104,200 using its observed $0.10 spread. These are fixture arithmetic, not market-price claims.

**Retraining and promotion.** Run weekly offline jobs with immutable cutoffs and a pinned recipe; do not promote weekly by rereading the same final holdout. Use chronological session groups, actual label-maturity intervals and at least 30 minutes embargo, extended for longer outcomes. Session-separated intraday labels often have no boundary overlap; still test the interval logic. Purely past-to-future folds do not need a fictitious “future training” embargo. A row-based sklearn `gap` cannot replace session/event-interval logic. Within each 12/3/1 development block, the first two validation months select hyperparameters/threshold and the last calibrates uncertainty. Hold the model frozen within each test month. After development, freeze hyperparameters, threshold and all recipe choices; monthly automatic coefficient/normalizer/calibration refits may use only matured past data in the registered windows, including earlier test months when they enter those windows. Thus the final six months evaluate a frozen adaptive recipe, with no human access to results or retuning until the predeclared end. They are not a static-model experiment. Weekly jobs do not activate new champions inside test months. Future recipe changes require fresh evidence. Promotion/rollback occurs only between sessions and always validates bytes and all compatibility hashes.

Data/feed/Greek/schema changes immediately disable new entry unless a compatible validated bundle is available. Keep a frozen healthy champion after a failed challenger; otherwise fall back to cash. Prediction drift alone is an alert, not proof of loss of edge. After 20 new sessions with ≥30 observed selected-policy outcomes, assess forecast residuals and actual quote-reconstructed cost drift. Disable when two consecutive scheduled checks have an upper 99% session-block bound for mean realized-minus-predicted return below the negative frozen adverse allowance. Independently disable if measured 95th-percentile submission latency exceeds the five-second envelope over ≥100 orders/attempts, or five consecutive stale/unknown-quote decisions reveal an unapproved feed degradation. These are proposed stop rules, not calibrated universal constants; preregister them and test their false-alarm behavior on development data. Hard risk/integrity failures disable immediately without waiting for a sample window.

## 5. Revised implementation architecture and typed interfaces

Use ordinary frozen dataclasses/enums/tuples and pure functions. `Decimal` is USD money or USD per share as explicitly named; option quantities are exact integers; booleans cannot masquerade as integers; all admitted datetimes are aware UTC. Stable JSON representations include versions and hashes. The records below define the minimum required boundary, not a request to build a generic trading framework. Existing validated fields can be retained where they satisfy this contract.

## 7. Validation program and objective gates

All numeric floors/tolerances in this section are proposed preregistration choices. They are deliberately falsifiable, not an assertion that 500 trades or a 95% interval guarantees profitability. If history or observations are insufficient, the result is **inconclusive**. Never make trades to satisfy a count or relax risk caps to reach statistical power.

|---|---|---|
| Unit / boundary | Every high/medium audit finding; hand-computed prices, dates, features, money and ties. |100% prescribed cases pass; exact Decimal arithmetic; stable rejection precedence; malformed/future data audited; no required failure silently becomes a valid quote or order. |

Inference unit is primarily the **session**, not every overlapping five-minute candidate label. Use 5/10/20-session blocks for development/final portfolio P&L, disclose dependence assumptions and sensitivity; the conservative conclusion must survive reasonable choices. All trial variants enter development multiplicity accounting, including thresholds, contract windows and hand-selected exclusions. The final artifact is a single frozen recipe, not a daily best-of-many retrospective model. Weekly jobs generate reports/candidates only; monthly activated coefficient/normalizer/calibration fits use matured past labels under the fixed recipe. Later recipe changes need fresh prospective evidence.

Count and report all planned decision slots, candidate attempts, observed fills, no-fills, censored exits, safety exits, cash decisions and rejected orders. Denominators matter. Report trade expectancy, median, tails, hit rate, coverage, turnover, drawdown duration, time under water, calibration, and results by month, time of day, side, DTE and volatility regime. Baseline comparisons use the same calendar/session-start capital and daily dollar P&L; cash's zero variance/PF are marked undefined. A cash baseline cannot be beaten by inventing trade-level expectancy for days it does not trade.

At most3 entries/session gives at most 90 entries in 30 paper sessions. Even 252 OOS sessions permit only 756 entries before abstention; the 500-trade floor may require a longer prospectively fixed observation period. Predeclare the final end date before unblinding; insufficient eligible full sessions/trades at that date mean inconclusive and a new prospective protocol, not an extension until significance appears. Before unblinding, estimate detectable effect size from development session variability and simulation/bootstrap power under the chosen error budget. The floor never replaces that precision calculation. Report cases where no eligible contract fits capital and distinguish “strategy infeasible at this capital” from “signal has no edge.”

Technical invalidators: future availability leakage; altered historical revision; adjusted deliverable ordered as standard; unverified artifact; new order on stale approval; unresolved or duplicated quantity; exit blocked by entry risk; unrecordable rejection; production endpoint reachable. These stop the relevant release immediately.

Economic invalidators: performance disappears at full ask-to-bid costs or delay; conservative lower bound<=0 at the preregistered test; model fails identical-cost baselines; censored-exit pessimism erases gains; monthly concentration/drawdown gates fail; signal depends on hindsight chain filtering or one regime; sufficiently powered fresh test refutes the predeclared minimum effect. An underpowered interval straddling zero is inconclusive, not proof of no signal and not permission to promote.

## 8. Red-team conclusion and prioritized actions

The strongest economic objection is that the platform may correctly forecast a small underlying move while paying more in option spread, theta and adverse selection than the forecast is worth. The forecast must also get IV repricing and timing right. Cash is a difficult baseline when every entry pays a spread. Liquidity observed just before deciding can disappear after submission; fills are selected outcomes, not random samples. A single liquid ETF and a short available history provide little independent regime evidence despite many rows. One-contract risk may make the admissible universe empty at the intended capital. Paper fills can conceal every one of these weaknesses.

The research may also fail because its required data cannot be obtained with the proposed budget and rights, because current and historical Greeks differ, because optional features consume flexibility faster than they add information, or because repeated weekly selection exhausts the untouched evidence. Perfect software cannot resolve those problems. The absence of a literature result for this exact policy is not proof of failure, but it places the burden on a new falsifiable experiment.

**Continue** if licensed data are attainable, a feasible one-contract universe exists at declared capital, operational tests pass, and fresh policy-level evidence survives adverse costs with useful precision. **Change direction** if good forecasts cannot beat option costs (test a distinct underlying-only hypothesis offline), if data support only slower horizons (new preregistered target), or if affordable eligible contracts are too sparse (different research scope through a design revision, never weaken current caps through configuration). **Stop promotion** if rights/history cannot be established, leakage or optimistic-fill dependence explains the result, or a sufficiently powered fresh test fails the preregistered economic gates. Keeping the platform as a reproducible negative-result laboratory remains a valid outcome.

**Before coding — highest priority**

1. Adopt/review the proposed observation, policy-label, entry authorization, recovery and audit contracts; resolve every High finding at the specification level.
2. Declare virtual capital, precise feature/Greek semantics, target denominator, score/cash rule, cost units and experiment budget. Preserve all existing risk caps.
3. Record endpoint-level data/rights feasibility; freeze paper-only and operational-versus-economic artifact classes. Start fixture core work even if economic data remain unavailable.

**Before backtesting**

1. Obtain admitted genuine historical quotes/reference history and underlying inputs with appropriate rights for economic evaluation; otherwise label the entire experiment plumbing-only.
2. Implement common feature and execution-policy replay, censoring ledger, quote-only LEAN fills, source-time tests and deterministic adapter harnesses.
3. Freeze chronological folds, actual label purging/embargo, all baselines, trial ledger, cost stress, confidence/power method and final untouched period.

**Before paper orders**

1. Pass core/replay/stress and 10-session shadow gates; prove endpoint confinement, account entitlements, current data/Greek readiness, idempotent reservations and restart reconciliation.
2. Demonstrate independent exits, cancel/late-fill recovery, early-close deadlines, durable risk latches and audit-failure behavior.
3. Enable only a clearly labeled permitted-use paper artifact; observe at least 30 sessions and the required execution sample without forcing trades or using paper fills as training truth.

**Before considering any future live deployment**

1. Require a separate project/design and explicit authorization; v1 continues to contain no live-order route.
2. Require new independent genuine-quote evidence, credible executable-cost/latency measurement, adequate precision and capital feasibility, plus a separately validated operational record.
3. Reverify then-current broker/exchange/regulatory obligations, exercise/deliverable handling and data/model-use rights. A paper or research gate alone can never authorize real-money execution.
