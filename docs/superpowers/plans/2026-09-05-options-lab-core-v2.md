# Phase 1: OptionsLab Verified Decision and Risk Core Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Core implementation is authorized; paper orders remain separately gated. Deliver the tasks through the small behavior increments below.

**Goal:** Build and test the broker-neutral core that computes shared features, evaluates call/put/cash decisions, enforces risk and exit policy, replays policy outcomes, reconciles identified order events, and records both valid and rejected decisions durably.

**Architecture:** Immutable typed observations enter one shared orchestrator. It evaluates safety exits independently of entry readiness, derives features from completed normalized bars, selects candidates, compares conservative one-contract dollar outcomes with cash, and applies risk. A separate shared submission gate revalidates a proposed entry against current observations/account state and atomically reserves its identity. No network transport, broker client, fitted model, or executable trading runner is part of this phase.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+, standard library `dataclasses`, `decimal`, `datetime`, `zoneinfo`, `enum`, `hashlib`, `json`, `sqlite3`, `tomllib`, and pytest for development. No numerical, broker, LEAN, HTTP, or pricing-library runtime dependency.

**Spec:** Adopted design in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-spy-options-paper-system-design-v2.md`; full rationale and typed architecture in `docs/reviews/2026-09-05-spy-options-review.md`; detailed audit in `docs/reviews/2026-09-05-interface-audit.md`. The original plan is superseded. This plan is self-contained for the Phase 1 tasks below.

**Phase boundary:** Freeze the revised observation, session, feature, prediction, policy-label, risk, exit, order-update and audit schemas in this phase, with deterministic contract fixtures. Implement only the shared rules and fixture-driven policy replay. Historical downloaders/training, LEAN simulation, Alpaca shadow/paper transport and licensed news/social each remain separate dependent phases. No paper-order or economic-performance readiness is claimed from Phase 1 completion.

## Global constraints

- SPY only; one long call or put; exactly one contract for every version-one entry, including after the first 30 paper sessions. No short positions, spreads, 0DTE, planned exercise, borrowing, overnight positions, or real-money execution.
- Inclusive entry window 10:00–15:00 America/New_York; one decision per five-minute slot. Submission must occur inside that window while original quote/intent freshness remains valid. The order lifetime ends at the absolute original decision time plus five seconds; cancellation wins at equality and fills must occur strictly before expiry. Delayed submission never restarts that clock. No submission after 15:00. Normal exit trigger is complete entry fill plus exactly 30 minutes. Safety exits can occur earlier.
- Authoritative XNYS strategy sessions; no entries on holidays or early closes. Regular liquidation begins at 15:35, escalates at 15:39, and requires confirmed flat/no opening orders by 15:40. On early closes retain `min(configured_start, close−25 min)` and `min(configured_deadline, close−20 min)`, with escalation one minute before that deadline. A failed liquidation is a recorded failure, never a fabricated flat state.
- Eligible entries require 7–21 calendar DTE, absolute delta 0.40–0.60 with correct right/sign, positive uncrossed quote sides, and spread `<= max($0.05, 0.08 × midpoint)`.
- Preserve or tighten these risk bounds: with `K = 100 × original decision ask cap`, every entry must satisfy `K + max($1.00, configured round-trip fee floor, applicable round-trip fee estimate) + max(0.005 × K, 100 × current option spread) <= 0.005 × virtual equity`; stop new entries at conservative session P&L `<= −0.01 × session-start equity`; stop new entries at drawdown `>= 0.05`; at most three filled entries per session; one pending entry or one open position, with no overlapping entry/exit authorization.
- The full left-hand side is both the capital reservation and the unchanged 0.5% risk-cap input. The adverse return floor remains 0.005. Never use a future exit spread in a decision or capital reservation. Report base, adverse and severe stateful execution/cost outcomes separately; policy changes create a new hash and require revalidation.
- A configured fee floor above $1.00 replaces the $1.00 floor in every reservation/cost formula; tightening the existing estimated-round-trip-cost setting cannot be silently ignored.
- Genuine live quote age is at most five seconds. A completed historical bar is an interval observation; its end time cannot stand in for an unknown quote update timestamp. Fill-forward, Indicative/delayed, and unavailable source-time observations retain those facts and cannot acquire executable-data status.
- Risk/market/account/clock/schema failures block entries. They never suppress evaluation of exits for an existing or uncertain position. Risk latches survive process restart; changing the active model to cash never resets them.
- V1 has no production endpoint or network submission capability. Simulated/replayed order commands have no side effects. Later transport phases must prove paper-account and endpoint restrictions independently of `paper_only=True`.
- News/social are absent from decision schemas. Optional research features cannot silently alter the active feature tuple or transform hash.
- Every public class/function follows the repository's Javadoc-inspired Python documentation conventions. Frozen dataclasses validate exact scalar types; money uses finite Decimal; all operational datetimes are aware and serialized in UTC. Raw failures remain representable as rejection events.
- Follow a failing-test, minimal-implementation, passing-test cycle for each task. Commit only intended changes after verification. Do not modify the LEAN engine.

- **Observed event time** describes the source event. **Available time** is the earliest verified time the decision pipeline could use it. **Received time** describes this ingestion. Retrospective download time does not replace historical availability.
- **Policy ID** hashes target definition, latency/cancel/exit rules, costs and feature/selection definitions. **Config ID** additionally identifies the effective operational/risk settings. **Bundle ID** hashes model bytes and its complete manifest. Check all applicable identities.
- **Primary outcome** is the return/P&L produced by the actual fixed one-contract policy. **Quote return** is the diagnostic ask-to-bid change at a fixed observation horizon. Never report the latter as a proven fill.
- **Halt** is a persisted latch or a recoverable pause with an explicit reset rule. Cash is an entry decision, not a command to ignore an existing position.

Canonical naming follows the companion review. `MarketSnapshot` below is a descriptive alias for `DecisionContext`, not a second schema. `ModelBundleManifest` is the serialized manifest payload; `VerifiedBundle` is the immutable descriptor produced only after validating that manifest and model content. Internal `DecisionResult` describes scoring; public `EntryEvaluation` includes candidates, features, forecasts and risk. `engine.evaluate_step` is the outer clock/order/market-event orchestrator and calls the canonical `decision.evaluate_entry` only after independently evaluating exits. `risk.authorize_entry`, `lifecycle.reduce_order`, `labels.build_policy_label` and `Predictor.predict(vector)` are the public names; do not create competing policy implementations under aliases. The vector carries contract identity.
